At the downtown Portland USGS gauge, negative discharge and velocity indicate upstream (reverse) flow; positive readings indicate downstream flow. Tides can temporarily reverse the Willamette. Velocity is measured at Broadway Bridge, not Sellwood. Local current can differ. USGS measurements are provisional and are not a paddling safety assessment. View official USGS station ↗
